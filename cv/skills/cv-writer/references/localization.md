# CV localization by market

Conventions that change between markets. Apply the row for the country of the job (or of the company, if the
role is remote for a foreign company). If unsure, use "International (English)".

| | Poland | International (English) | UK / Ireland | US / Canada | DACH (DE/AT/CH) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Document name | CV | CV / Resume | CV | Resume | Lebenslauf |
| Length | 1–2 pages | 1–2 pages | 2 pages | 1 page (2 for senior) | 2 pages |
| Photo | optional, common | no | no | **no** | optional, still common; never required |
| Date of birth, marital status | no | no | no | **never** | optional, usually left out now |
| Data-protection clause | yes, if the ad asks or by default for Polish employers | only if the ad asks | no | no | no (only if the ad asks) |
| Dates (RenderCV `locale`) | `wrz 2024` via `locale-pl.yaml` | `Sept 2024` (`english`) | `Sept 2024` | `Sept 2024` | `german` |
| Spelling | — | British or US, consistent | British (optimise, organisation) | US (optimize, organization) | — |
| Tone | formal, factual | factual, results | understated, factual | strong verbs, numbers up front | formal, complete, no gaps without explanation |
| Extra | — | — | — | — | cover letter (Anschreiben) often expected; place, date and signature at the end is traditional, optional |

## Data-protection clauses

Polish (standard):

> Wyrażam zgodę na przetwarzanie moich danych osobowych dla potrzeb niezbędnych do realizacji procesu rekrutacji
> zgodnie z Rozporządzeniem Parlamentu Europejskiego i Rady (UE) 2016/679 z dnia 27 kwietnia 2016 r. (RODO).

Polish (also future recruitments, only if the ad asks or Filip wants it):

> Wyrażam również zgodę na przetwarzanie moich danych osobowych w celu przyszłych procesów rekrutacyjnych.

English:

> I consent to the processing of my personal data included in this application for the purposes of the
> recruitment process in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).

German (rare; only if the ad asks):

> Ich willige in die Verarbeitung meiner personenbezogenen Daten für Zwecke des Bewerbungsverfahrens gemäß DSGVO ein.

If the ad contains its own clause text, use that text verbatim instead.

## Language levels

Use CEFR (A1–C2) everywhere in Europe; in the US add a word ("Polish — native, English — fluent (C1)").

## Other languages

French, Spanish, Dutch, Nordic and others: write in English unless the ad is in that language. If a CV in such a
language is really needed, produce it, keep sentences simple, and tell Filip it should get a native check.
Europass format only when the employer asks for it (EU institutions, some public tenders).
